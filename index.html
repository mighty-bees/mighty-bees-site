Our first season.

The Mighty Bees are the robotics team at Centner Academy Middle and High School in Miami. We're Team 31800 in the REC Foundation's Engage league. We build VEX IQ robots and compete against schools around South Florida. This year's game is Tier Takeover. Here's the manual.

We have one robot kit, some LEGO the old club left behind, and a couple of 3D printers. Everything else we have to raise. Right now we're all busy getting a new club off the ground and we could use some resources.

$0 raised of $1,680 $1,680 to go

That's an estimate. It covers a second team registration, practice supplies like a field mat and bean bags, and a second robot if we get that far.

Tournament entry fees are separate. Those are collected per event from the families whose students are competing, and we'll ask as each one comes up.

    What we make

    Everything here is designed and produced by students on our 3D printers and laser cutter, made to order.

    Want one? Email us and we'll tell you how soon we can have it ready. We also take custom jobs. If you have your own file, send it over for a 3D print quote. If you need something that doesn't exist yet, tell us and the students will design it. Working to a real brief is the most useful thing they do all year.
