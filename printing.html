Get a 3D print quote.

We rent out time on the team's 3D printers. You send us the file and the details, we come back with what it'll cost and how long it'll take, and if you're happy with that we print it. Students do the work and every dollar goes to the robotics team.

Tell us about the job below. It takes a minute.

Step 1 of 2

About the job

So we can send the quote back.
What the part is and what it's for. A replacement knob, a prop, a prototype, whatever it is.
Length, width and height. A rough measurement is fine. If it's bigger than our print bed we'll tell you, and we can often split a model into pieces and glue them.
Running a batch of the same thing to sell? Put the number here and we'll quote the whole run. Tell us below if it's something you'll want again.
Optional. Tell us what you had in mind and we'll say whether we can do it.
Optional. Tell us if it's tied to a date and we'll say in the quote whether we can hit it.
Optional. Strength, finish, whether it has to fit something exactly.

Stuck, or this isn't working? Email team@mightybeesrobotics.org and just tell us what you need printed. No account needed either way, nothing is stored on this page, and we only use your email to answer you.

Step 2 of 2

Attach your file and hit send

Your email program should have opened with all of that filled in. Attach your .stl file to it (.3mf, .obj and .step work too), then send.

If nothing opened, press Copy the details instead above and paste them into an email to team@mightybeesrobotics.org, with the file attached.

No file yet, just an idea? Send it anyway and say so. Students can draw it up.

What we can't print

Nothing that holds food or drink, no weapon or weapon parts, and nothing a student shouldn't be handling. We'll say no to a job we're not comfortable with, and we'll tell you why.

Questions first?

Email team@mightybeesrobotics.org and ask. You don't have to fill in the form to talk to us.
